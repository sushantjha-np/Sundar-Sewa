A registered corporate firm built on trust, expertise & results.

Sundar Sewa Pvt. Ltd. is a government-registered corporate firm providing a wide range of legal, financial, accounting and digital services across Nepal. Established in 2079 BS (2022 AD), we help businesses and individuals navigate everything from consultation to complete business operations.

Our team brings together expert lawyers, certified auditors, qualified accountants, licensed insurance agents and skilled support staff — collectively holding more than 15 years of professional experience. We also offer internship opportunities for students who want to learn, practise and grow under an experienced team environment.

Whether you need business registration, company updates, share capital restructuring, book keeping, tax consultation, accounting, auditing or bank loan consultation — or a complete digital presence through website development, graphic design and website administration — we deliver reliable, compliant and competitive solutions.

Our office is located at Kalimati-13, Kathmandu, adjacent to Aincho Paincho Credit & Saving Corporation. Reach us anytime via WhatsApp, phone, email or social media — we are always ready to connect.
